FOUNDER & MANAGEMENT

Founder & Head

Mr Ganesh Mandem B.Tech (Ind)| M.S. (Italy) | Ph.D. – CY (Ongoing)

Founder and Head, The Mandem Meritorship Awards

I am deeply connected with ZPHS Nooliveedu, as I studied here from 6th to 10th standard. This rural and remote school gave me the foundation, values, and confidence that shaped my journey. Coming from a humble background, my educational journey was not always easy. Like many students from rural areas, I travelled several kilometres, walked long distances, and depended on bus journeys to continue my studies. The support of my parents, teachers, and the determination to learn helped me move forward. In 2014, I had the privilege of becoming the first student in the history of our school to achieve the highest academic score from its establishment until that year and the first student from our school to secure admission into IIIT. I was fortunate to receive the Andhra Pradesh Government Prathibha Award, along with scholarships and recognitions from various organizations. These encouragements inspired me to give back to the institution where my journey began. The Mandem Meritorship Awards is a small effort to recognize and encourage the talented students of our school. The purpose is not just the reward, but the confidence, motivation, and inspiration created among students. I believe talent exists everywhere, including government schools. With proper encouragement and opportunities, every student can achieve great heights. My sincere appreciation to all the parents, teachers, and the Government of Andhra Pradesh for their efforts in strengthening government education. "A small encouragement at the right time can inspire a lifetime of achievements." "Life teaches us — giving after becoming successful is charity, but helping while growing is gratitude. When someone needs support, even a small help at the right time can make a great difference."
